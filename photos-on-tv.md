# Photos on TV: choose the pictures and start the slideshow

Your selected photos fill the TV. Your phone stays available for everything else.

## Get the result

1. Open [vuthis.com](https://vuthis.com) in the TV browser.
2. Scan the display's QR code with your phone, or enter its code at [vuthis.com/r](https://vuthis.com/r) from a laptop or tablet.
3. Choose **Photos**, select your pictures and tap **Show now**.
4. Use the photo controls to change pictures, or turn **Slideshow** on to advance automatically.
5. Choose **Fit** to keep the whole picture visible or **Fill** for an edge-to-edge display.

## Make it easy to repeat

Keep a selected set for family photos, a trip recap, a club gathering or a meeting. The display receives the content you choose rather than a live copy of your whole phone or desktop. The controller and display can use different internet connections.
