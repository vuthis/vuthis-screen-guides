# V U This screen walkthroughs

**PDFs, slides and photos on TV. Open a page, connect and show.**

Practical resources for home TVs, meeting-room displays, classroom projectors and workshops. Open [vuthis.com](https://vuthis.com) on the display; connect from a phone, laptop or tablet.

## Choose the result

| What you want on the screen | Walkthrough |
| --- | --- |
| A PDF on TV or a projector | [Show the PDF and turn its pages](pdf-on-tv-or-projector.md) |
| PowerPoint, Keynote or Google Slides | [Export to PDF and use your phone as the clicker](powerpoint-on-tv.md) |
| Photos or a slideshow | [Choose the pictures and show them](photos-on-tv.md) |
| A document or photos controlled from a laptop | [Connect the laptop controller without HDMI](laptop-to-tv-without-hdmi.md) |
