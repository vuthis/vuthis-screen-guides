Show a PDF on a TV or Projector Without HDMI

Your document on the big screen. The page controls in your hand.

The room sees your PDF. You keep the page controls on your phone or laptop.

Get the result

  1. Open vuthis.com in the display browser. For a projector, use its browser or the computer already driving it.
  2. Scan the display's QR code with a phone, or enter the code at vuthis.com/r on a laptop or tablet.
  3. Choose Document, select a PDF and tap Show now.
  4. Use Previous and Next to turn pages.

Choose Fit page to keep a complete slide or diagram visible. Choose Fit width to read a tall page a screenful at a time.

No app installation or HDMI cable from your controlling device. Both devices need internet access; they can use different networks.

Pick the right view for the room

Start with Fit page for a slide, floor plan or diagram that needs to stay visible in full. For a portrait document, try Fit width so the text is larger. Readability matters more than squeezing every page onto the screen at once.

For a meeting, keep the agenda or proposal ready on your controlling device before connecting. For a lesson or workshop, open the worksheet first and check it from the back of the room.

Does the projector need its own browser?

No. A browser on the computer already connected to the projector can supply the display page. Leave that page visible while you connect from a separate phone, tablet or laptop.

Can I show a PDF from a MacBook or Surface?

Yes: open the browser controller, enter the screen code and choose Document. The device you control from does not need an HDMI connection to the display. Keep both devices online while using the session.
