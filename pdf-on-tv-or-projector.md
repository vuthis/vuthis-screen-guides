# PDF on TV or projector: your phone turns the pages

The room sees your PDF. You keep the page controls on your phone or laptop.

![A PDF on the display with its page controls beside it](https://vuthis.com/assets/pages/display-pdf-on-tv-from-phone.jpg)

## Get the result

1. Open [vuthis.com](https://vuthis.com) in the display browser. For a projector, use its browser or the computer already driving it.
2. Scan the display's QR code with a phone, or enter the code at [vuthis.com/r](https://vuthis.com/r) on a laptop or tablet.
3. Choose **Document**, select a PDF and tap **Show now**.
4. Use **Previous** and **Next** to turn pages.

Choose **Fit page** to keep a complete slide or diagram visible. Choose **Fit width** to read a tall page a screenful at a time.

No app installation or HDMI cable from your controlling device. Both devices need internet access; they can use different networks.
