# Put a PDF or photos on TV from a laptop without HDMI

The TV shows your selected document or photos. Your laptop holds the controls.

## Get the result

1. Open [vuthis.com](https://vuthis.com) in the TV's browser.
2. Open [vuthis.com/r](https://vuthis.com/r) in your laptop browser and enter the code shown on the TV.
3. Choose **Document** for a PDF or **Photos** for selected pictures.
4. Select the file or photos and tap **Show now**.
5. Change pages or pictures from the laptop.

Use a PDF export when showing PowerPoint, Keynote or Google Slides. For a projector, the display page runs in its browser or a browser on the computer already driving the projector.

The controller and display both need internet access. They do not need shared Wi-Fi pairing. This workflow sends chosen supported content to the display; it is useful when the room needs a document or pictures quickly.
